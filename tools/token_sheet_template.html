What to judge

  • Does each outline read as its own unit, or only as a 'Mech of that weight? Only the Timber Wolf has an override so far. Silhouette, torso and head come from the chassis-name hash (the model number does not change them); arms, weapons and jump jets come from the file.
  • Arms come from the file: hand, forearm, or a gun-pod arm. Weapons sit by zone and class (barrel, rack, turret, port), not by slot order.
  • Torsos, heads and legs are the hash's guess. Every unit that looks wrong is a candidate for a new parameter value or a named override.
